Research Background & Problem

Modernizing Road Infrastructure with Intelligent AI

Traditional road maintenance in Sri Lanka relies on manual visual audits, subjective damage estimates, unscientific asphalt tonnage calculations, and uncoordinated lane closures that create severe traffic gridlock. Road Inspector AI solves this with a unified, 4-stage cyber-physical platform.

Research Motivation

To eliminate the dangerous gap between visual defect detection and actual physical road maintenance execution. By bridging deep learning computer vision directly with civil engineering standards (CIDA SCA/5, RDA HSR, ASTM D6433), municipalities can budget and execute repairs with mathematical precision.

  • Automated Defect & Risk Detection: Replaces manual walking audits with multi-scale YOLOv8 + WBF.
  • Exact Pixel Repair Area & 3D Depth: Moves beyond crude bounding boxes to True Shoelace area and Depth Anything V2 volume.
  • CIDA-Compliant Material Thermodynamics: Thermodynamic cooling curve modeling ensures HMA compaction temperatures above 135°C.
  • Weather-Aware Scheduling & Detour Bypass: Genetic NSGA-II optimization avoids monsoon rain & morning school traffic peaks.
